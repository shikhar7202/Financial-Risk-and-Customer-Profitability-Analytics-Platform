# Financial Risk & Customer Profitability Analytics Platform
**Industry:** Banking / Credit Cards / FinTech | **Stack:** SQL, Python, Statistics, Power BI

## Business problem
Balance customer growth, profitability and credit risk.

## Decision framework
Low risk + high profit → Premium / cross-sell
Low risk + low profit → Nurture
High risk + high profit → Monitor / reprice
High risk + low profit → Reduce exposure

## KPIs
Default Rate, Risk Score, Utilization, Revenue, Profit, Profit per Customer, Delinquency Rate.

## Pipeline
CSV → SQL risk-profit segmentation → Python analysis → Power BI decision dashboard.
